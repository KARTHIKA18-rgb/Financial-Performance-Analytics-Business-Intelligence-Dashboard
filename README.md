# 📊 Financial Performance Analytics & Business Insights 

## 📌 Project Overview

**Financial Performance Analytics & Business Insights** is a data analytics project focused on analyzing financial performance across different businesses and business sectors.

The project uses **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SQL, and Power BI** to clean, analyze, visualize, and transform financial data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on important financial indicators such as **Revenue, Profit Per Unit, Operating Cost, ROA, ROE, ROIC, P/E Ratio, Dividend Yield, Sales Growth, and Profit Growth** to evaluate profitability, operational efficiency, business growth, and investment performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall financial performance.
* Compare financial performance across business sectors.
* Identify high-revenue and high-profit businesses.
* Analyze operating cost and its impact on performance.
* Evaluate ROA, ROE, and ROIC across sectors.
* Understand sales and profit growth patterns.
* Analyze financial valuation metrics.
* Detect potential outliers in financial data.
* Identify relationships between financial metrics.
* Build KPIs for business performance monitoring.
* Generate actionable business insights for decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** `Financial_Performance(2).csv`

| `SalesGrowth_Pct`       | Sales growth percentage                                   |
| `ProfitGrowth_YTD_Pct`  | Year-to-date profit growth percentage                     |
| Other financial metrics | Additional financial performance and valuation indicators |

# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

Multiple visualizations were created to understand financial performance from different perspectives.

## 1. Profitability by Business Sector

The analysis compares the average **Profit Per Unit** across different business sectors.

### Insights

* Profitability differs considerably across sectors.
* Some sectors consistently generate higher profits.
* Profit distribution varies between industries.
* High-profit sectors can indicate stronger business returns.
* Low-profit sectors may require operational improvements.

---

## 2. Revenue by Business Sector

Revenue was analyzed across business sectors using interactive visualizations.

### Insights

* Revenue varies significantly across sectors.
* Some sectors contribute substantially higher revenue.
* Revenue is unevenly distributed across industries.
* A limited number of sectors contribute a significant portion of overall revenue.

### Business Understanding

High-revenue sectors can be prioritized for future investment, while low-performing sectors require further analysis to identify revenue improvement opportunities.

---

## 3. Revenue vs Operating Cost

Revenue and operating costs were compared to understand cost efficiency.

### Insights

* Operating costs generally increase with revenue.
* High-revenue businesses can also have higher operating expenses.
* Some businesses maintain comparatively lower operating costs despite generating high revenue.
* Cost efficiency varies considerably across businesses.

### Business Understanding

Effective operating-cost management can improve profitability and provide a competitive advantage.

---

## 4. Average ROE by Business Sector

**Return on Equity (ROE)** was analyzed across business sectors.

### Insights

* ROE differs significantly between sectors.
* Most sectors demonstrate positive ROE.
* Some sectors show negative ROE, indicating weaker profitability or inefficient use of shareholder capital.
* Several sectors demonstrate relatively high ROE, indicating strong equity utilization.

### Business Understanding

Businesses with consistently strong ROE demonstrate better utilization of shareholders' funds.

---

## 5. Average ROA by Business Sector

**Return on Assets (ROA)** was analyzed to understand how efficiently businesses utilize their assets.

### Insights

* ROA varies across business sectors.
* Most sectors show positive ROA.
* Some sectors have negative ROA.
* High-ROA sectors demonstrate better asset utilization and operational efficiency.

### Business Understanding

ROA can be used to compare how effectively businesses convert their assets into returns.

---

## 6. ROIC Distribution

The distribution of **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)** was analyzed using a histogram.

### Insights

* Most companies have ROIC values concentrated around the moderate range.
* The majority of businesses generate positive returns on invested capital.
* Some companies show very high ROIC values.
* Negative ROIC values indicate businesses that are not generating sufficient returns from invested capital.
* Extreme ROIC values appear as outliers.

### Business Understanding

ROIC is useful for evaluating how efficiently businesses allocate and utilize invested capital.

---

## 7. P/E Ratio Distribution

The **Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio** was analyzed to understand market valuation.

### Insights

* Most companies have moderate P/E ratios.
* The distribution is positively skewed.
* A small number of companies have exceptionally high P/E ratios.
* High P/E values may indicate strong growth expectations or premium market valuations.

### Business Understanding

P/E should be compared across companies within similar sectors when evaluating relative valuation.

---

## 8. Dividend Yield by Business Sector

Average dividend yield was compared across sectors.

### Insights

* Dividend yield varies considerably across sectors.
* Many sectors show moderate dividend yields.
* Some sectors have significantly higher dividend yields.
* Certain businesses have very low or near-zero dividend yields.

### Business Understanding

High dividend-yield sectors may be attractive to income-focused investors, while low-dividend businesses may be retaining earnings for future growth.

---

## 9. Sales Growth Distribution

Sales growth was analyzed using a violin plot.

### Insights

* Most businesses show moderate sales growth.
* A smaller number of businesses demonstrate exceptionally high growth.
* Some businesses experience negative sales growth.
* The distribution highlights differences in business expansion performance.

### Business Understanding

Businesses with consistent positive sales growth can be considered strong growth performers, while negative-growth businesses may require strategic improvements.

---

## 10. Profit Growth YTD Distribution

Year-to-date profit growth was analyzed to understand recent profitability trends.

### Insights

* Most businesses show positive profit growth.
* Profit growth is concentrated around moderate positive values.
* Some businesses experience negative profit growth.
* A few companies demonstrate exceptionally high profit growth.

### Business Understanding

Consistent profit growth can indicate sustainable business performance, while negative growth requires investigation into costs, revenue, and operational efficiency.

---

## 11. Top 10 Businesses by Revenue

The top 10 businesses were identified based on revenue.

### Insights

* Revenue is concentrated among a small number of high-performing businesses.
* One or a few businesses significantly outperform others in revenue generation.

# 🔎 Outlier Detection

Box plots were used to identify unusual values across numerical financial variables.

Outliers were observed in metrics such as:

* ROA
* ROE
* ROIC
* Operating Cost
* P/E Ratio
* P/B Ratio
* P/S Ratio
* Sales Growth
* Cash Flow Growth
* Dividend Yield

### Important Observation

Not every outlier represents an error.

Some outliers may represent:

* Highly profitable businesses
* High-growth companies
* Premium-valued companies
* Businesses with exceptional dividend yields
* Companies experiencing unusual financial performance

Therefore, outliers should be investigated before automatically removing them.

# 🔗 Correlation Analysis

A **Correlation Heatmap** was created using Seaborn to understand relationships between financial metrics.

### Key Findings

* Most financial variables show relatively weak correlations, indicating that they measure different aspects of business performance.
* **ROA and ROIC** show a strong positive relationship.
* **ROA and ROE** show a moderate positive relationship.
* **ROE and ROIC** show a positive relationship.
* **Profit Growth YTD and P/E Ratio** show a moderate positive relationship.

### Business Understanding

The correlation analysis helps identify which financial indicators tend to move together and provides a better understanding of relationships between profitability, efficiency, growth, and valuation metrics.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

The overall analysis provides several important insights:

1. Revenue and profitability vary significantly across business sectors.

2. High revenue does not necessarily guarantee high profitability.

3. Operating cost management is an important factor affecting business performance.

4. Businesses with strong ROA and ROIC generally demonstrate better resource utilization.

5. ROE helps identify businesses that efficiently utilize shareholders' capital.

6. Sales growth and profit growth help identify businesses with strong expansion potential.

7. Financial valuation metrics such as P/E, P/B, and P/S vary considerably between businesses.

8. Some businesses show exceptionally high financial performance and can be used as benchmarks.

9. Negative growth and negative return metrics highlight businesses requiring further investigation.

10. Outlier analysis helps identify both exceptional performers and potentially unusual financial conditions.
